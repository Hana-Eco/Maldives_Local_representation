Descriptive assessment of peer reviewed marine science papers from the Maldives from 1985 to 2025. The assessment was paired with a description of the history of Maldivian marine science for context. 

Out of 662 candidate papers, 534 papers were assessed and 524 papers that met the inclusion criteria were analysed. The 534 assessed papers are provided in the "Paper coding dataset".   

The list of 128 papers that were not assessed are provided in the "List of unassessed papers.xlsx" 

Codes used to produce the figures within the manuscript are provided within the codes subfolder.
