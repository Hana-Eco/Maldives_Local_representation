# Affiliation is not participation: local authorship and inequalities in 40 years of Maldivian marine research   
### *Paper coding dataset DOI*:
### *Timeline dataset DOI:*

Descriptive assessment of peer reviewed marine science papers from the Maldives from 1985 to 2025. The assessment was paired with a description of the history of Maldivian marine science for context. 

Out of 662 candidate papers, 534 papers were assessed but only the 524 papers that met the inclusion criteria were analysed. 

* The list of the 128 papers that were not assessed are provided in "List of unassessed papers.xlsx"
* Codes used to extract data and detailed in "Coding descriptors.xlsx"
* Codes used to produce the figures within the manuscript are provided within the codes subfolder.
* The "Paper coding dataset" includes data from all the assessed papers. The 524 papers that met the inclusion criteria and were analysed were sub setted from this main dataset.
